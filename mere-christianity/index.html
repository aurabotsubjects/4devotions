1 · The big picture · 2 min

Where does our sense of right and wrong come from?

If everything is just atoms, instincts and evolution, why do we get so angry when someone cuts in line?

Say this

Lewis starts his whole book here. He says our sense of fairness is a clue, like a footprint, that points toward who made us and what he is like. Today we follow that clue.
